Our love story

A beginning worth remembering

Our story starts with a Whatsapp status update but really, it begins with a show we still find ourselves coming back to every winter - Game of Thrones.

We were only in our 2nd year of university, still so young, and somehow already looking for home in each other. Our paths crossed, diverged and crossed again as if there was a force gently tugging on us to keep finding each other.

Fostering our connection during a global pandemic was not easy but we always say it’s what made our foundation even stronger. Bonding over long phone calls (that our families complained about), movie/series suggestions and debriefs and whatever contentious topic was trending on the socials.

Once united, we just knew that this time our paths had finally converged into one, gently tugging us to our forever.

We hope you can join us on 28.11.2026, to celebrate a new beginning of our journey with each other.
